### Added
- A folded turn that is still working keeps its newest tool row on screen, so the fold never hides
  what the agent is doing now.
- The header's ⋯ menu can hide the pinned todo list, and turn off its folding so the list stays
  open.

### Changed
- The agent's plan and OMP's unfinished subagents now sit together in a rail beside the
  conversation, open on arrival, instead of a dock under the transcript; a narrow pane docks the
  rail under the transcript. The plan is open by default.
- The Agents list is a tree: each workspace heads its own agent rows, which leave the place to the
  head, and a head folds. A switch at its head groups the same rows by coding agent instead
  (agent kind, then its workspaces, then the agents); the choice is shared. The devices and the
  Agents tree now share the sidebar three to seven instead of capping the tree at half. The
  sidebar's edge and the chat rail lose their rules, and the workspace tree indents its rows
  without a guide line. The sidebar lists, the chat transcript, the chat rail and the earlier
  sessions sheet scroll without a visible scrollbar.
